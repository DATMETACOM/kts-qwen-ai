# SF11 EWA Demo Script

## Opening

Problem: salary-based borrowers still submit payslips and bank statements manually, which slows approval and increases fraud risk.

Solution: connect to employer payroll systems, verify salary in real time, and offer either on-demand earned wage access or payroll-linked loans.

## Walkthrough

1. Start at the hero metrics.
   - highlight payroll verification
   - lower turnaround time
   - low NPL ambition through payroll-linked repayment
2. Move to employer readiness.
   - show active integrations
   - call out payroll freshness in hours
3. Click 2 contrasting employees.
   - one strong EWA case
   - one higher-risk loan case
4. Show reasons behind approval and decline rules.
5. Close on portfolio controls and implementation roadmap.

## Demo Message

This PoC is not just a front-end concept.
It demonstrates the operating model of payroll-verified underwriting, consent gating, and automatic exposure caps for responsible salary-linked finance.
