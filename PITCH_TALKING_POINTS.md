## 🔑 Key Talking Points by Project

### SB10 - Branch Traffic Prediction
**Problem:** Customers don't know wait times, leading to:
- Long wait times (20-30 min average)
- Peak hour congestion (11am-1pm)
- Poor staff allocation

**Solution:**
- AI-powered hourly traffic forecast
- Best Time to Visit recommendations
- Real-time queue management

**Metrics:**
- Reduce wait time by 50%
- Improve staff efficiency by 40%
- Customer satisfaction +30 NPS

**Demo:** Show dashboard with 5 branches, forecast for today

---

### SF8 - Customer Behavior Prediction
**Problem:** Thin-file customers (no credit history) are difficult to assess
- Manual income verification takes days
- High NPL from poor targeting

**Solution:**
- Alternative data scoring (telco, e-wallet, e-commerce)
- Qwen AI generates personalized offer script
- 4-layer privacy pipeline

**Metrics:**
- New customer acquisition +40%
- NPL reduction 30%
- Processing time: days → minutes

**Demo:** Show customer pipeline with scoring

---

### SF11 - EWA & Salary-Linked Lending
**Problem:** Employees need emergency cash before payday
- 3-7 day loan approval TAT
- Manual payslip verification
- High acquisition costs

**Solution:**
- Real-time payroll verification from HRM
- Instant EWA (up to 50% of earned salary)
- Salary-linked loans with auto-debit

**Metrics:**
- NPL target < 2% (via auto-debit)
- TAT: 3-7 days → 5 minutes
- Cost reduction: 40%

**Demo:** Show 3-portal system (Employee, HR, Admin)

---

### SF12 - MicroBiz Loan
**Problem:** Digital economy workers (freelancers, gig workers) can't access traditional loans
- No salary slips
- Irregular income patterns
- New market segment

**Solution:**
- AI-powered micro loans (5-50M VND)
- Alternative credit scoring from cashflow
- Revenue-linked repayment (% of revenue)

**Metrics:**
- Target NPL < 5%
- New customer segment: gig economy
- Flexible repayment lowers risk

**Demo:** Show seller/platform/admin portals
