# SF12 MicroBiz Loan Demo Script

## Opening

Problem: digital sellers and gig workers often have healthy cash flow but weak traditional credit files.

Solution: score real operating revenue from marketplaces and wallets, then structure repayment as a percentage of revenue so the loan breathes with the business.

## Walkthrough

1. Start with the headline KPIs.
   - show target segment expansion
   - show alternative scoring rationale
   - show revenue-linked repayment advantage
2. Review partner channel readiness and payout freshness.
3. Open one low-risk and one high-risk seller case.
4. Stress the scenario controls.
   - tighter risk appetite
   - weaker seasonal revenue
   - higher revenue-share cap
5. Close on monitoring signals and rollout path.

## Demo Message

This PoC shows how Shinhan can move beyond payslip-based lending and capture underserved digital income segments with explainable alternative scoring and flexible collections logic.
